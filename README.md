# 🏦 Agile BA Sprint Showcase

There is a lot of noise online about what a Business Analyst actually does. This project cuts through that noise to show you[cite: 2].

---

### 📉 The Friction Point
A mobile-first Dutch digital bank was watching one in three potential customers walk out the door during identity verification[cite: 2]. The underlying system wasn't broken; the experience was[cite: 2]. The upload journey had been copy-pasted directly from an old 2021 web design to mobile, left without an operational owner, and stripped of an active SLA[cite: 2]. Instead of guiding users, the application fired a cold, generic error message that permanently scared away 65% of customer drop-offs[cite: 2].

This portfolio documents the exact trace a Business Analyst leaves behind when diagnosing this chaos—transforming a massive customer drop-off loop into clean, strategic, and developer-ready logic[cite: 2].

---

## 🛠️ The Operational Artifacts

### 📂 [01 - Business Problem Statement](./01-business-problem-statement)
**The Analytical Anchor.** This is where we stop guessing and look at the raw numbers. It maps out the core issue, calculates the commercial stakes (€2.1M in revenue exposure and €313K in customer service bleed), and defines our baseline success criteria before anyone writes a single line of code[cite: 2].

### 📂 [02 - Stakeholder Register](./02-stakeholder-register)
**The Risk Mitigation Map.** Six key organizational actors mapped tightly by power, interest, and daily motivations[cite: 2, 4]. It balances a UX designer’s creative requirements with a Compliance Officer's non-negotiable legal vetoes, utilizing a structured RACI matrix to kill political bottlenecks before sprint planning[cite: 2, 4].

### 📂 [03 - AS-IS Process Map](./03-as-is-process)
**The Diagnostic Teardown.** A detailed BPMN 2.0 blueprint tracking the broken experience across three operational swimlanes (Customer, System, and Compliance)[cite: 2]. Annotated with data to point out the precise failure loops wrecking the funnel[cite: 2].

### 📂 [04 - User Stories + Acceptance Criteria](./04-user-stories)
**The Technical Translation.** Ten highly structured, sprint-ready user stories written through a customer-centric and compliance-focused lens[cite: 2, 3]. Engineered via strict Behavior-Driven Development (Given/When/Then criteria) and ruthlessly prioritized using the MoSCoW framework to completely eliminate developer ambiguity[cite: 2, 3].

### 📂 [05 - Jira Sprint Board](./05-jira-board)
**The Backlog Machine.** Sprint 1 built, loaded, and estimated[cite: 2]. This translates the business logic into active engineering workflows, showing exactly how requirements convert into progress[cite: 2].

### 📂 [06 - TO-BE Process Map](./06-to-be-process-map)
**The Future State Optimization.** The future-state workflow where the failure loops are systematically replaced with automated 4-point systemic pre-validation gateways and real-time photo quality tracking, successfully squeezing a 4.2-day compliance queue into a tight under-24-hour SLA[cite: 2].
